Primary source anchors

These sources guide review. The relevant statutory tests and business facts must be assessed before any obligation is assigned.

Delaware corporate filings

Delaware Division of Corporations ↗

Confirm corporation versus LLC and formation records before assigning filings or deadlines.

Delaware alternative entity filings

Delaware LLC, LP and GP tax instructions ↗

Alternative entities follow different filing rules from corporations. The app entity's legal form is unconfirmed.

Delaware privacy applicability

Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act ↗

Assess statutory scope, thresholds and exemptions against actual processing and residents. Distinguish provisions in force on 5 October 2026 from future-effective provisions.

California privacy applicability

California Attorney General CCPA guidance ↗

Resident locations, business thresholds, processing and exemptions need a separate assessment. Delaware incorporation does not resolve California scope.

Children and teens

FTC COPPA business guidance ↗

Under-13 applicability and teen safeguards require separate analysis; an age gate alone is not evidence of compliance.

Health and nutrition

FTC Health Breach Notification Rule guidance ↗

Establish actual health data and data-source combinations before deciding coverage.

Health app roles

HHS health app guidance ↗

HIPAA scope depends on the relevant entity and relationship. A nutrition feature alone does not establish HIPAA applicability.

Consumer health data

Washington Attorney General health data guidance ↗

Assess Washington scope against the actual data and consumer relationships. Do not equate consumer health privacy with HIPAA alone.

Biometric identifiers

Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, section 15 ↗

Determine whether processing creates covered identifiers or information. Video alone does not establish applicability.

Payments and value transfer

FinCEN money services business guidance ↗

A wallet label does not establish regulated activity. Document custody, transfers, redemption, jurisdictions and providers for specialist review. Contest and prize law needs a separate jurisdiction review.

Security controls and assurance

NIST Cybersecurity Framework ↗

Security framework use and SOC 2 or ISO 27001 assurance do not become mandatory merely through Delaware incorporation.
